A mixing console
in your pocket.

Mischpult is German for mixing console. It is a two-deck DJ app for Android with the controls of one: waveforms, sync and key lock, hot cues and loops, effects, a sampler, turntables to scratch on, MIDI controllers and set recording. Free and open source.

What's on the console

Two decks

Scrolling waveforms you can grab like a record, coloured by bass and treble. Tempo with key lock, automatic BPM and beat grid, one-tap sync.

Mixer

Gain, three-band isolator EQ with full kill, a one-knob filter, channel faders and a crossfader with smooth and cut curves.

Cues and loops

Eight hot cues per track, saved with it. Auto loops, loop rolls and beat jumps that stay on the grid, sample-exact.

Effects

A beat-synced ping-pong echo, reverb and flanger per deck, with tails that ring out after you switch them off.

Scratching

Turntables that follow your finger at 33⅓ rpm, flicks that coast and slow down like a platter, and a cut fader that jumps to your thumb.

Sampler

Sixteen pads in four banks, quantised to the leading deck. Grab a deck's loop onto a pad, or load a file; key lock keeps its pitch.

Key detection

Every track's key in Camelot notation. Keys that mix with what's playing light up green.

MIDI controllers

USB and Bluetooth LE controllers with MIDI learn, jog wheels, performance pads and LED feedback.

Record the set

REC records the master to your Music folder, with a set list of what was on air, ready to share.

Headphone cue

Split output sends the master to one ear and the cue mix to the other: pre-listen with a splitter cable.

Low latency

A native audio engine on Oboe: a few milliseconds from touch to sound on recent phones, and it keeps playing in the background.

Gig ready

Prepare a crate ahead of time so the set never depends on the venue's network. Portrait, landscape and tablets.

Your music, your sources

Mischpult plays the audio files on your device. It contains no code for any streaming or video service. Online sources can be added as separate source plugins: apps that talk to Mischpult through a small, documented protocol. A plugin brings its own code, licence and responsibilities.

Install

  1. Download the APK from the latest release on your phone.
  2. Open it. Android asks once whether your browser or file manager may install apps; allow it.
  3. Open Mischpult, go to Library → Files and add the folder with your music.

Or build it yourself: ./gradlew :app:installDebug — see the README.
